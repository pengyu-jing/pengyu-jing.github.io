Pengyu Jing

Ph.D. Student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore

I work on robot learning and robotic manipulation, with an emphasis on reinforcement learning, vision-language-action models, simulation, and real-robot systems.

I received my M.Sc. in Robotics from the National University of Singapore and my B.Eng. in Robotics Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China.

Goal: Build reliable robots that can learn, adapt, and improve through real-world interaction.
